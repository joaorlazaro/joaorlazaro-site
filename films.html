Films

My Video Editor Reel and a selection of eight films: fiction, documentary, video art, participatory work and a music video. Everything else is on the Work page.

Tale of the Witch, 2024

Assistant Camera, Editor. Directed by Catriona Burnett.
Two siblings looking for a lost phone end up in a comic, magical quest to prove which of them is the better witch. ● Tees Valley International Film Festival 2024. → Short Films

The Holiday When Julia Was Without a Phone, 2020

Director, Videographer and Editor.
A mockumentary about finding new ways to interact with the world beyond screens. ● I National Film Festival of Chapecó 2021. → Short Films

Livre Reportagem #3 [Free Report #3], 2018

Director, Videographer and Editor.
Testimonies recorded with passers-by in the centre of Florianópolis. ● Young Artists Award, Contemporary Art in Santa Catarina. → Livre Reportagem

Unwanted Attention, 2026

Writer, Director, Videographer and Editor.
One-minute horror for Newcastle Film Club's Micro-Horror challenge, July 2026. → Short Films

Falling by Gwil, 2024

Director, DoP and Editor.
Music video. ● Best Overall Music Video, Newcastle Film Club. → Short Films

The International Year of Artisanal Fisheries and Aquaculture 2022, 2022

Coordinator of the collaborative coverage; Videographer, Editor and Workshop Facilitator.
Made with a team I trained during a seminar that brought fishing representatives from 16 countries to Ilha do Cardoso, São Paulo. → Artisanal Fisheries

Em Obras [In Works], 2021

Editor. Directed by Gustavo Zinder.
Documentary on the restoration of the Hercílio Luz Bridge, Santa Catarina's most important architectural monument. → Em Obras

Vida.com [Life.com], 2016

Workshop Instructor and Videographer.
Documentary made with school students in Educom.cine, on access to public health around their school, including teenage pregnancy. → Educom.cine

Filmmaker, editor and educator based in Newcastle upon Tyne, working in community filmmaking, documentary and educational media.
